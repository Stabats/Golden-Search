Property

   Upon coming to the island, your character will register themselves as a citizen of the town. They will be given the surname Hills-Willow to be used on the island and a simple plot of land for them to live on. They will be able to work with the locals to expand their plot and help recover the land that has been wrecked by disasters on the Island.

   Each player will begin with a simple studio home on their starting plot. The size of your home is tied to how many items you are able to use during a chapter. The starting home grants you 1 item slot. Your item slot will allow you to use any item in your inventory with the "Chapter" tag either on yourself or one of your teammates. In order to upgrade your home, you will need to meet the required pinkies and land size listed on each upgrade.

   Players will begin with 1 block of land that is 5ft² big and will be able to expand to own 400 blocks of land. Players can unlock the ability to expand the amount of land they have by completing the Property Rights quest. Once that is complete, you can then spend your pinkies to keep upgrading your plot of land. You will be able to see how much land you have unlocked on your player spreadsheet.

   The Island also faces much of the same instability as your home worlds have been facing. As part of the story's progression, players will need to restore the land on the Island through gardening and resource collection. In order to collect resources, players will need to complete quests to earn them and then contribute them to the total. In order to contribute plants, players will need to spend pinkies on restoring their owned land.

Player Spreadsheet

   Each player will recieve their own spreadsheet that they use to manage their land and crafting items. There will be two tabs on it, Land & Crafting. These two sheets are tied to your inventory so when you perform actions on these sheets, it will update your inventory on the website. There will be an Action Menu located in your breadcrumb next to the Help menu. It will have the options Craft, Sell & Land available for you to use. You may need to tell Google that you allow scripts when using it for the first time.



   In addition to the Action Menu, there are also buttons on the sheets that you can click to run the scripts as well. On the Land sheet, it's the image of the plot of land. On the Crafting sheet, it's the image of a cutting board for crafting & an image of a pinkie for selling.

   If on mobile, you need to open your spreadsheet link in your browser and swap it to desktop mode in order. Make sure the link isn't in html mode (you will know if you can't edit & it lists html at the end of the link, you can simply delete the html part to refresh it).


   The left side of your Land sheet will have a color key, how many Pinkies you currently have & if you have a Landowner's deed. Once your deed is listed as yes, you will be able to purchase and/or buy plants to restore your land. Basic land costs 5 pinkies, planting on land you already own costs 20 pinkies & buying land already planted costs 25 pinkies. In order to spend money on your land, you will:
  • Select the block you wish to edit. Only do one at a time. It can be any block between the 20x20 grid.
  • Click on Action Menu -> Land. Follow the prompts & it will then run your purchase based off your answers.

   Your Crafting sheet will display how much of each ingredient/dish you have, the results of your crafting & a log for any addition of items given to you. You don't need to interact with any of the blocks on this sheet, solely select either Craft or Sell either from one of the buttons or from the Action Menu.
