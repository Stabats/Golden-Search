Building Your Home

Players start off with their own plot of land which they can use to build their own home on. These homes can be customized and upgraded through land, buildings, furniture, and decorations. Players have full freedom over the design of their house inside and out, they simply need to purchase the materials to do so.

Landowner's Deed

A landowner's deed is an item that states a piece of land belongs to a character. A player obtain this item in order to own and purchase land. In order to gain this item, the player will need to complete the following quests:
⇢ "Welcome to the Land"
⇢ "Property Rights"
Once these quests have been completed, the player will recieve the item and once it's confirmed in the inventory, the player can start building and upgrading their land.

Plots

There are a large variety of plots avaiable for purchase that players may use to their heart's content. However, you can only own one size plot in town at a time. Therefore, when you purchase a new plot, you are basically upgrading their current plot size. Each plot is a square where the smallest is 5 feet by 5 feet (1.5 meters by 1.5 meters) and the largest is a 100 feet by 100 feet (3,048 meters by 3,048 meters). The type of ground the plot is up to the player e.g. grassy, dirt, stone, mixture, etc. Different size plots are required as certain requirements.

Houses

There are 6 different types of housing the player can choose from to build on their plot of land. In order to build a house on a plot of land, the player needs to have the required land area and building materials.
