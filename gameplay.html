Gameplay

Participating

   As an ARPG, the group’s story progression will be tied to not just roleplay but other extra curricular activities mainly through art. Players are expected to work together to complete different objectives to move the story along. As such, any character in the group will be expected to be a willing teammate & work together with others. Characters do not have to be social butterflies, happy with their circumstances, etc. however, they must be willing to cooperate with their team & not sabotage them without OOC consent from your fellow players.

   The roleplay will primarily take place on the forum organized by chapters, interims & general. Players will be split into assigned teams by the mods and will work with that team for the majority of the chapter. There will be some times when all players are together in the same space and can freely interact then. Otherwise, you will only have your team to interact with as you progress through the chapter. Each team will be provided a cellphone that they can use to send a single message to any 1 team per checkpoint. Use this wisely as you may need it for completing puzzles on your adventure…

Tasks
   Players will also need to work together to complete additional tasks. There will be different goals the players must partake in such as completing an art piece for a quest or spending money to buy certain items. These additional tasks will primarily take place in the form of quests during interims where completing quests may unlock new things for the players. Quests can range as simple as turning in an art piece you did on your own to receive an item for yourself or be a collective submission goal where the quest is completed a certain amount of times by the players to be considered successful. Sometimes there will be smaller goals in the chapter roleplays themselves such as paying money to an NPC to cross a bridge. Don’t have enough money to pay the bridge toll? Well, it seems you’ll need to figure out together how to solve that issue…
Actions
   Throughout the roleplay, each character will be allowed to make 1 action per post per midpoint/checkpoint. You are welcome to post as many times as you would like, however, you cannot take another action until the next midpoint/checkpoint has been posted. You are encouraged to work with your team IC and OOC to ensure you complete any action necessary to complete an objective. You may use magic as flavor so long as it does not count towards completing the objective. For example, Andra uses her magic to turn a rock to dust. She may do so without consuming an action/not needing to roll. However, if she were to use that dust to blind someone in the same post, she would need to take an action/roll.
Activity
   Due to the collaborative nature of the campaign, players will need to track the amount of posts they do in each chapter. A post will count to their total if there is a minimum of 3 sentences in it. Players will be expected to reach a minimum of half the chapter’s checkpoints to receive the participation bonus. For example, if the chapter has 10 total checkpoints then the players will need to reach 5 posts to be legible for the bonus. The bonus will be 200 pinkies and an additional 10 pinkies per post after reaching the initial requirement.

   Players are welcome to take a break/haitus whenever they need to should the need arise. They will not be punished for missing out on chapters and only removed from the campaign if they so wish to. In order to participate in a chapter (after the first), you will need to sign up for it when the call goes out. You will go to your respective ticket to let the mods know you are signing up for the next chapter along with your current stats (as these stats will be added to your character’s page on the masterlist & expected to be followed in that chapter). Once a chapter begins, you will not be able to change your character’s stats.

Stats

   The group will utilize stat rolls to influence outcomes of events or actions your character partakes in. Each character will have a total of 5 stats tied to themselves: Dexterity, Strength, Charisma, Will and Sociability. The higher a stat is, the more likely you will succeed in your actions. There will be opportunities for players to upgrade their stats during the campaign.
   Players will have control over the 3 main stats a character has and will pick their character’s starting amount for each of them. Each of the 3 main stats will be on a scale of 1-10 where 1 is the lowest you can have and 10 is the highest. Each of these stats will grant you a modifier to add to your rolls as bonus to showcase your proficiency in a stat. These modifiers are determined by the stat number as follows:

    1-2: +0
    3-4: +1
    5-6: +2
    7-8: +3
    9-10: +4

   You will have a starting pool of 10 points to allocate between these 3 stats at the start. The 3 main stats are as follows:

    Dexerity (DEX)
       Any physical movement beyond your natural state. Things like jumping, climbing, dodging, swimming, etc. will fall under this stat.
    Strength (STR)
       Any physical action with force. Things like lifting, physical attacks, pushing, etc. will fall under this stat.
    Charisma (CHA)
       Any social action done with NPCs. This stat will influence chances for deception, intimidation, persuasion, performance, animals, cooperation, insight and NPC friendship.

   Each character’s Will stat will be the stat that influences any action you perform with your magic. The stronger the Will, the stronger your magic is. It will also work as your defense stat. The lower the Will, the stronger the magic will have over you. Be careful now… having a low Will when going up against an opponent with an elemental advantage may not land in your favor.
   At the start, everyone will begin with 1 point in Will. Over the course of the campaign, this stat will increase as players unlock the next levels in their magic. Each character will have 7 Will by the end of the campaign while players may earn items to increase the Will to the full 10 if they so choose. This stat uses the stat itself as its modifier.
   Sociability stat determines how welcome on the island you are. As outsiders, it is your job to blend in with the locals so you can complete your mission without causing too much suspicion. Characters with high SOC will have an easier time interacting with NPCs. Characters with a low SOC stat will experience a harder time doing things such as:

    Unable to interact with certain NPCs
    Lack of cooperations from NPCs
    Hostility / attacks from NPCs
    Potential disciple issued by the NPCs such as jail time to be fulfilled

   Each character’s Sociability stat will be determined by the mods as per the results of your actions. The stat works on a scale of -5 to 5 and everyone will start on a clean slate of 0. Players will be notified at the time of when their actions result in a change in their SOC.
   As a collaborative campaign, your actions can potentially impact others. Characters seen with low SOC characters may experience a harder time with NPCs while ones with high SOC characters may experience an easier time. On top of this, such situations may change your SOC stat as a result so for example, higher SOC characters may be able to influence NPCs into liking lower SOC characters as a result. Your SOC will primarily work as either a blockade, a disadvantage, an advantage, or granting access.


Rolling

   In this campaign, players will be responsible for rolling for their actions and providing the results in their post. In the corresponding dice channel, players will use the bot to roll for them or they are allowed to use physical dice so long as a photo of the result is posted in replacement of using the dice bot. Rolls are composed of a rolling 1d20 block plus modifier. Some examples:

  1. Andra wishes to climb up a rock wall & her DEX stat is a 3. She will roll 1d20+1 for her action.
  2. Andra wishes to push a heavy crate out of her way & her STR stat is a 1. She will roll a flat 1d20 for her action.
  3. Andra wishes to ask an NPC a question & her CHA stat is a 6. She will roll 1d20+2 for her action.
  4. Andra wishes to use her magic to create quicksand & her Will is a 10. She will roll 1d20+10 for her action.
  5. Tia wishes to use her magic to mimic a bird’s call & her Will is a 3. She will roll a 1d20+3 for her action.

   When in a battle against something, you will need to do an additional roll called a Reaction roll. You may flavor your reactions as you wish in your post, be it either a dodge, block or a counterattack but in the end, it’ll all be the same roll. Reactions will determine if you are able to deal extra damage or avoid taking extra damage. Reaction rolls will be determined by your Will stat and will be rolled with a 1d20 block plus modifier. Some examples:

  1. Andra wishes to use her magic to throw a rock at an enemy & her Will is a 10. She will roll a 1d20+10 for her attack and a second roll of 1d20+10 for her reaction.
  2. Tia wishes to use her magic to slice an enemy with wind & her Will is a 3. She will roll a 1d20+3 for her attack and a second roll of 1d20+3 for her reaction.

   You may combine Will and 1 of the main stats together as one action. You will simply need to roll the respective block roll for each stat. You cannot combine 2 of the main stats together as an action nor can you use multiple main stats with your Will as an action. Some examples:

  1. Andra wishes to jump over a pit while using her magic to throw a rock at an enemy. Her Will is a 10 and her DEX is a 5. She will roll a 1d20+10 for her attack, second roll of 1d20+10 for her reaction, and a third roll of 1d20+2 for her jump.
  2. Tia wishes to use her magic to mimic a bird’s call to convince a bird to follow her. Her Will is a 3 and her CHA is a 10. She will roll a 1d20+3 for her magic use and a second roll of 1d20+4 for her persuasion attempt on the bird.

   Your success in an action or reaction will be determined as falling into a category: critical failure, loss, average, success, critical success. The categories are broken down as follows:

    Critical Failure
       Result of 1-6. True failure. Guaranteed results such as lowering SOC, taking double damage, missing a jump and taking damage, failing to persuade an NPC so they no longer speak to you that day, etc.
    Loss
       Result of 7-12. Failing an action but able to try again. Guaranteed results such as missing a jump, taking a hit, failing to persuade an NPC, etc. It has an additional 80% chance of lowering SOC.
    Average
       Result of 13-18. Mixed results. Guaranteed results such as succeeding in a jump but needing someone else to catch you, succeeding in pick pocketing an NPC but only being able to get a single coin instead of the whole purse, etc. It does not affect SOC in any way.
    Success
       Result of 19-24. Success but not a master. Guaranteed results such as succeeding in a jump, succeeding in pick pocketing an NPC, succeeding in persuading an NPC to tell you something, etc. It has an additional 80% chance of raising SOC.
    Critical Success
       Result of 25-30. True Success. Guaranteed results such as raising SOC, dodging any attack even if its elemental advantage, succeeding in convincing an NPC to tell you everything about something, doing double damage, not losing AP when taking action against an enemy, etc.

   Your character’s health will be known as Action Points (AP) here. Each character will have 20 AP to spend in a chapter. Your AP will be affected by combat and critical failure stat checks. Every time you perform an action against an enemy or take damage from an enemy or stat check failure, you will lose AP. The following scenarios are how you lose AP:

    Succeeding (13-24) an action against the enemy: -1 AP
    Failing (7-12) your action against the enemy: -1 AP
    Critically failing (1-6) your action against the enemy: -2 AP
    Failing (7-18) your reaction: -1 AP
    Critically failing (1-6) your reaction: -2 AP
    Using magic that costs AP to use as outlined in the power level used
    Having elemental disadvantage: -1 AP
    Critically failing a stat check: -1 AP

   These scenarios can stack when applicable such as succeeding in your action but critically failing in your reaction. The max amount a character can lose in one go is 9 AP: critical action fail (2) + critical reaction fail (2) + spending AP for a magic power (3) + elemental disadvantage (1) + critically stat check fail. You will be able to gain more AP either through the use of magic or consuming/using items. Items and healing magic will not cost AP so you are able to heal and still use an action. However, you can only use an item or magic in your turn and if you wish to have more points at once, your teammates can use their items/magic on you as well for combined healing. Any critical successes you get on your actions, you do not lose any AP as a result.
   In order to defeat an enemy, players will need to spend a certain amount of AP. For example, if an enemy has 50 AP then 50 actions need to be taken against the enemy. Actions taken against an enemy will be counted as a collective. For example, if an enemy has 15 AP then Player 1 can take 10 actions while Player 2 can take 5 actions and the enemy will be considered defeated. Enemies may be able to heal themselves just as the players can. Players will be expected to work together to defeat enemies throughout the story. The following scenarios are how enemies lose AP:

    Your action hits (13-24) them: -1 AP
    Your action critically (25-30) hits them: - 2 AP
    Your reaction succeeds (13-24) against them: -1 AP
    Your reaction critically succeeds (25-30) against them: -2 AP
    Successfully (13-24) hitting you: -1 AP
    Failing (7-12) an action against you: -1 AP
    Critically failing (1-6) an action against you: -2 AP
    Losing AP from a magic power as outlined in the powers sheet
