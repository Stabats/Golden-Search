Character Creation

Backstory

   Characters will come originally from another world that they can create. Please refer to the world page for more information.

   Your character will have arrived at Talliah'la Island by unknown means and will not remember how they got here. They will remember their life history and their home unless you wish to give them some kind of amnesia. They will remember they were going to go meet with someone unknown who promised to give them seeking the answer they most desire. How they heard about this someone can be through anything such as posters, world of mouth, tv ads, etc. Why they choose to go to this meeting or what they seek is entirely up to you. Either way, they will remember wishing to go to this meeting but not actually doing so. Everything after will be blank until your character awakens on the Island. Your character loses any personal belongings they had prior unless it was absolutely necessary (ie. hearing aids).

   Everyone will start in the town Hills-Willow after waking up on the beach. They would've been found by Andra and Tia who would've helped get them settled into the region and guide them. They've introduced your character to magic and prepare you to use it on your own. They will promise you that they will help you get back home once they figure out how.

   Age works the same as human years on the Island. Characters may be as young as 15 human years when arriving upon the Island. Characters are required to be a minimum of 18 human years old at the start of the campaign. Characters shouldn't be older than 70 at the campaign start.

Appearance

   No matter what you were before, you now have been turned into a cat. Cats primarily walk around on 4 paws but are able to switch to 2 paws. However, they can only stand on 2 paws for short periods of time where it lasts about half hour max at a time. Your character should plan on walking on all 4's more often than not.

   Some cats wear clothes that crafted to function in both positions. However, the culture here is only wear what is deemed necessary and most cats will normally have nothing on. Fashion is considered an art form here rather than a necessity, thus, resulting in luxury prices for all clothes even something as simple as a shirt. Characters may start with 1 piece of clothing (shirt, pants, cloak, etc.) OR 1 accessory (jewelry, bag, etc.) after they're brought to Hills-Willow. Characters cannot wear paint, skulls or feathers as accessories/clothes. Anything worn by your character cannot restrict their movement.

   Cats are primarily natural colors but may have unnatural accents in their pelts. You can have 1 unnatural color in the design and once you unlock your Primary Magic, you can add 1 more unnatural color based off your Primary. Please use colors not too close in value in your design so your cat's patterns are more visible. Patterns are typically natural but can have unrealistic shapes. Cats may have tufts of fur on their heads but it must be fur and not be wig-like. Braids can be present and eye color can be anything.

   Characters may have 1 small mutation such as small amounts of feathers, scales, gills, etc. If feathers are used, they need to be incorporated to be apart of the cat so they do not look like accessories and cannot be behind the ear. Characters cannot have huge changes in anatomy not seen in cats and cannot fly.

   Characters can be any domestic species of cat, domestic hybrid cat or wild cat. Characters can be a height of 7" - 20" at the top of their head. When walking on 2 paws, their height can be of 1ft - 3ft at the head. Characters should not have human anatomy such as having defined shoulder and should look like a normal cat standing up on their hind legs.

   Characters may have disabilities if you so wish, however, this should be done respectfully and with research. ASL is just as common as Spoken languages and the citizens are literate. There is also technology on the Island to provide aids and better access to things as well.

Application

   You will need to fill out a written and art portion for the application. The written application has a set format you must use which you can use the google doc or toyhouse version. Please ensure that the application is viewable! The art portion will take place of the group's art application sheet & the .psd file can be found in the Discord. Please remove any placeholder information found in the applications with your character's.

   You can find information on stats on the gameplay page. Backstories and appearances should follow everything listed up above. Once ready, you can submit your application here.
