Character Creation

Premise

Your character has arrived to Talliah'la Island by unknown means. For backstory, your character can be any species you desire. - Appearances can vary as you wish as long it makes sense to your world. - No one, NPC or player, can have any magical powers prior. There may be tales of magic wielders in the past but they are merely stories. - Wildlife may be known to have magic but are rare & their magic cannot be wielded by others. Here on Talliahla's Island, everyone is a cat no matter what they were before. - Fur color can be any realistic cat color. - Patterns can be any realistic pattern and have up to 1 unnatural shape. - Tufts of fur can be on cats' heads but must match the fur, no wig like shapes. - Braids are fine in long haired cats. - Cats can be any domestic cat breed and any domestic hybrid cat breed. Cats be also be wild cat breeds so long as they can naturally be the same size as the height restriction. - The height is measured by the head: 7" - 20". - Cats can walk on 2 paws for short bursts & have enough balance to stand to complete tasks that may require it. They can be a height of 1ft-3ft at the head. - Despite this, cats do not have humanoid anatomy such as defined shoulders. - Eye color can be anything, no matter the circumstances. - Characters may have a small amount of features such as scales, gills, feathers, etc. Needs to be one type of feature per character & should only be roughly 5% of the design. - Feathers can only be found in the following areas displayed on the cat model. - Age works the same as human years. Characters should be somewhere from 20-50. For belongings, your character has lost anything they had before including what was on them at the time. - Characters may start with 1 piece of clothing or accessory. Whatever is chosen cannot restrict movement. - Character cannot wear any paint or skulls. Feathers cannot be accessories. - Characters may gain more accessories/clothes through the shops. Keep in mind that nothing can restrict movement. - Civilians have their own taste in fashion where their preferences vary throughout the island. What may pass in one area may not in another... Be careful or else your sociability may be affected.
