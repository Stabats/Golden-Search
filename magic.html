Magic

The Universe's Magic

   The Universe is made up of various particles primarily known as atoms. Forming together, they can create anything with no true limit to what they can be. These formations are commonly called Elements and grouped into five main groups. These five groups are used to create primarily everything in the Universe (including yourself!) and can be possibly harnessed by their creations in the Universe as a result. Magic is the art of bending the Elements to the user's will. Magic can be as simple as creating a campfire using wood and match box to changing the weather. Magic is a natural phenonmon and tool widely used in every timeline. How Magic presents itself can differ through the timelines as how the Elements present themselves may differ but the core of the Elements will always remain the same. Here on Talliah'la's Island, players will be interacting with the core of the Elements when performing Magic.

   On the Island, residents commonly use Magic to make their lives easier in more advanced ways such as creating fire from "nothing" rather than using matches. Technology on the Island is a reflection of this as using Magic instead of a tool is more common but tools still exist especially for those that aren't as advanced in their Magic. Residents normally only learn their Affinity and go through basic schooling for this to ensure no one "accidently triggers" their Magic due to how much easier it is perform Magic here. Sometimes, resident may learn other than their Affinity but they normally only keep to the basics in everything including their Affinity. Residents don't normally learn anything more than level 3 in any Element due to the dedication it takes to go beyond that.

Magical Impressions

   A Magic phenomenon on the Island, more commonly known as ghosts in other timelines, are called Impressions. Sometimes, a piece of someone who's dead may be left behind and manifest as if they hadn't died. They're transulcent and are the color of one of the Elements. Are they really back from the dead? Hard to say... However, they seem to be a more frequent occurrence than anywhere else.

   Impressions on the island differ from those found in other worlds. They seem to be a lot more colorful as well as if they're made of multiple Elements rather than just one. At times, Impressions are able to appear as if they're perfectly normal living being rather than as their magical translucent form. They seem to be tangible enough to interact with the world around them including creatures. They display a minor intelligence, are able to communicate, and even seem to have a sense of who they once were. However, they aren't who they fully used to be and seem to grow agitated to the living if they're present for too long... They're capabale of wielding unpredictable magic along with being able to deal physical damage. Citizens are advised to leave Impressions and any strangers they don't know alone so they don't end up being attacked. As such, Islanders don't travel or wander very often.

Magic Sigils

They say eyes are doors to the soul.

   A by product of using Magic will be a Sigil that will appear in some form on the enviroment around the user, typically where the effect took place. The Sigil will appear depending on the level of the user and demand of the spell. The more of a "master" you are in an Element, the less your sigil will appear when performing effects. Your sigil will always appear for levels 5 & 6.

   Players are free to design their sigil themselves. Sigils will need to be a simple closed shape and can have max 2 different shapes used. Sigils color will be based off of eye color where if heterchromia is present, there can have 2 colors. The one on the left is an example of 1 eye color and 1 simple closed shape based sigil. The one on the right is an example of heterochromia present with two simple closed shapes present. Sigils may have a small hint of the Element color present when performing an effect. The colors are: Brown for Earth, Red for Fire, Blue for Water, Green for Air and Purple for Aether. They aren't present during effects that mix Elements.


The Universe's Elements

   There are 5 different branches of Magic. These come from the Elements that make up the Universe: Water, Fire, Earth, Air and Aether. Living beings (players, plants, animals, etc.) are made of all the Elements put together in different ways. A character's Primary magic is the Element that they will primarily be made of and will be assigned to you. This will be your affinity element and will come easier to wield. Characters will also have a secondary magic that they get to pick themselves.

Earth

The very ground you stand on.

This element encompasses anything tangiable made from earth. Rocks, pebbles, sand, metal, ash, bones and charcoal are some of the things that can be manipulated. Any element from the periodic table is also fair game as long as it's a tangiable form. Higher level users can use any type of Earth can be manipulated and shift states of matter along with creating earth from "nothing" using the periodic table.

Water

Demise in 3 days without this.

This element encompasses anything made of H2O. Anything from a water source, vapor/steam, dew, rain and plants are some of the things that can be manipulated. Higher levels users can shift states of matter and create water from "nothing" by using the hyrdrogen and oxygen or humidtiy around them.

Fire

A destructive or nuturing wrath.

This element encompasses any flame, smoke, heat and healing. Flames created from anything such as gas or metal can be manipulated freely. Higher level users can have any color, be any temperature, and seemingly create it from "nothing" by using their own body's resources. Despite it's destructive nature, a trained user can easily heal any wound with fire and heat.

Air

The very oxygen you breathe.

This element encompasses anything made of the gases found in the atmosphere or any natural elemental gases. Air can be manipulated around the user to even manipulate sound. Higher level users are known to be able to mimic sounds or even defean them.

Aether

The essence of the universe that's kept in the background.

This element encompasses anything made of energy, light and gravity. Aether users can manipulate energy such as lightning, change the lighting in a place or change the gravity to create floatness. The element is known to mainly manipulate what is directly around them but can create by spending their own energy.

The Elemental Flow

The way the universe changes.

   While the Elements can work together in harmony, sometimes they clash in their more pure state. When using magic, users manipulate the raw core of the Element to perform to the user's will. Stronger the will, the easier to bend the Elements to your whim. However, due to this state of usuage, Elements will clash upon contact with other Elements. Elemental Effects

   When using Magic, you don't only manipulate the elements around you but also yourself. It is through yourself that you use Magic thus resulting in yourself clashing with the Element of use. Side effects can be seen as accidently burning a finger a bit from a lit match or possibly becoming too hot from being near a fire for too long. These effects will become more apparent to you as you manipulate the core of the Elements such as draining your energy so you always feel tired. Lower levels will have smaller effects compared to higher ones and as such, it's important to only "master" your affinity Element. Attempting to "master" an opposing Element will result in that Element gradually overcoming you until you fragment as your being fights to not allow the Element to overpower you. Eventually, it will stalemate to the two becoming wisps of their former selves and consumed back into the atoms of the Universe.

Elemental Levels

   Wielding the core of the Elements is difficult and requires effort on the users' part to "master" Magic. As one becomes more fluent performing Magic, they will be able to do things more easily and do more complex things. Each Element has levels from 0 to 6. Every level 0 ability is available to any character to use no matter what kind of magic it is. In order to go past LVL 0, that Element must be your primary or secondary Magic. A full list of powers can be found here.

   Characters will start at lvl 1 in their Primary Magic and lvl 0 in the others. Your Primary Magic will reach up to LVL 6 and LVL 3 in their Secondary Magic. Learning multiple Elements will allow players to use Magic in more complex ways such as combining elements. Primary Magic progression will be unlocked throughout the story while Secondary Magic progression will be unlocked through quests. You can find these quests here.
