Questing

Quests

   Quests are tasks that players can do to earn rewards and help drive forward their own personal story or the main storyline in a smaller way. Most quests are supplementary activities to do during interims for players to have things to do while a chapter isn't being run. They are completed by creating a piece of writing, art or roleplay about your character doing the objective of that quest. Each quest will have a different prompt for you to solve and may have extra stipulations needed in order to complete that quest. Unless specified otherwise in the quest description, every requirement needed to complete the quest is:
  • Art: 1 polished fullbody
  • Writing: 200 words
   Players can collab on quests with other players if they so wish, however, the requirements are doubled:
  • Art: 2 polished fullbodies
  • Writing: 400 words
  • Roleplay: 4 posts per person
   Rewards for completing a quest can range from items for your character to use, pinkies, badges, and/or unlock new things. To submit your completed quest to claim your rewards, you can do so by turning it in for pinkies.

Pinkies

Pinkies are the currency used in this world. The cats used to trade using mice as payment and in particular, baby mice which are known as pinkies. It has since evolved into the official currency used on Talliah's island in the form of coins.
As a player, you can earn pinkies OOC through art & writing. Requirements for submissions are:

  • Any art/writing must fits the setting of the group
  • Is drawn/written by you
  • Tracing, bases, stolen work or AI is banned
  • Follows any other rules of the group.

The amount earned per piece is determined by the total of the options featured in each piece. The following are some examples on how much different pieces are worth.

Simply fill out the corresponding form to your completed piece and a mod will add your earnings to your inventory!

Art Submissions

Fill out the following form to submit your artwork for pinkies.

Art Grading Form
Art Completion

Draft: 0 pinkies

  • Any art that doesn't fall into any of the other completed bonuses. It still counts for pinkies but doesn't receive a bonus for being a draft.
Clean bonus: 20 pinkies
  • Art with linework and color.
Polished work bonus: 30 pinkies
  • Art with linework, color, & shading. May have a simple background (shapes, pattern, single color, etc.)
Full piece bonus: 40 pinkies
  • Art with linework, color, shading, & scenic background

Character Size

Headshot: 10 pinkies

  • Drawn work of the head of a character with or without the neck.
Halfbody: 20 pinkies
  • Drawn work that features at least 40% of a character in some way.
Fullbody: 30 pinkies
  • Drawn work that features at least 80% of a character in some way.

Bonuses

Gift bonus: 30 pinkies

  • Art made featuring another user's character. Doesn't stack with collabs.
Collab bonus: 20 pinkies
  • Art/writing made with another player in unison. Doesn't stack with gifts.
Animation bonus: 30 pinkies
  • Moving pictures!
