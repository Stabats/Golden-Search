Questing

Quests

   Quests are tasks that players can do to earn rewards and help drive forward their own personal story or the main storyline in a smaller way. Most quests are supplementary activities to do during interims for players to have things to do while a chapter isn't being run. They are completed by creating a piece of writing, art or roleplay about your character doing the objective of that quest. Each quest will have a different prompt for you to solve and may have extra stipulations needed in order to complete that quest. Unless specified otherwise in the quest description, every requirement needed to complete the quest is:
  • Art: 1 polished fullbody
  • Writing: 200 words
   Players can collab on quests with other players if they so wish, however, the requirements are doubled:
  • Art: 2 polished fullbodies
  • Writing: 400 words
  • Roleplay: 4 posts per person
   Rewards for completing a quest can range from items for your character to use, pinkies, badges, and/or unlock new things. To submit your completed quest to claim your rewards, you can do so by turning it in for pinkies.

Catalogue

Pinkies

   Pinkies are the currency the citizens of the island use to exchange goods. It started as using actual mice (pinkies) by the cats but then evolved to used coins instead but kept the name. Different variations of the coins with various values assigned to them have happened over the years until the current set was established. Pinkies come in 4 different types of coins with different values:
  • Pink worth 5
  • Silver worth 25
  • Bronze worth 50
  • Gold worth 100
   Pinkies are no longer in production as society has moved to a digital era for currency instead. However, there are still vendors on the Island who will still accept the physical coins in exchange. The coins have become a legacy item and now a well sought after by collectors who gladly take them in instead of digital. The pink coins are the most sought after out of all the coins because despite their technical lower value, the pink ones are the ones with printing variations.

   Pinkies will also be the currency the group uses that players can earn as rewards from their participation, art and writing. You can find more on how to earn pinkies from your art & writing on the submissions page.

Badges

Badges are the achievement system within the group and players can earn these by participating in various ways in the group. Players will be earn these badges as a sign of their accomplishments and hard work in the group. Players will be able to view which ones they've earned so far in the group in their inventories. To claim any badges outside of chapters or pinkie submissions, simply submit them in your ticket.
