Rules

Overall

These rules apply to everywhere in the group whether it be the discord, forum, etc.

   Play Nice.
      Please be kind and respectful to your fellow peers. If someone asks you to stop talking about something, please drop the topic and move on. Please include CW & spoil things that are on the spoiler list. Anything on the banned list should not be discussed anywhere nor included in character histories/roleplay. Do not harass others and discrimination towards others for their race, gender, etc. isn't allowed. Please do not discuss drama as this is a place for relaxtation & fun! Do not talk poorly of other roleplays particularly by name. Should any issues arise with other players, please try to resolve it between one another civilly but if need be, feel free to reach out to the mod team for help.

   Group is 16+.
      Players are required to be a minimum of 16 years old in order to join the group. The group contains violence, injury, death and explicit language. Players are allowed to swear so long as they aren't using it directly on others. Players are expected to interact with the group when sober and should not do so when under the influence.

   Avoid minimodding.
Players can answer simple questions for others if the answer can easily be found such as it being listed on the website. If you're unsure on something, just wait until a mod comes to answer it instead. Players cannot be answering questions for others in the help channel.

   No AI or plagerism.
Any generative AI for art, writing, character bots, etc. will not be allowed to be used in the group nor allowed to be shared. Please do not steal / copy / reference other people's designs, art, writing, etc. This is a place to use YOUR creativity, not someone else's!

Roleplay

   Be a good sport.
Please do your best to not take in character conflict too close to heart. Not every character will get along perfectly and just because a conflict occures doesn't mean it is a personal attack upon you. Please stay civil out of character with one another and do your best to sort out boundaries with one another if need be.    No godmodding.
Please avoid controlling the narrative, controlling others' characters, refusing to let your character have reasonable flaws, etc. Please communicate with your team if you have ideas on things you'd like to do that may heavily affect others. Do your best to include others in the roleplay rather than focusing too much on yourself. Sometimes a character needs a moment but you should do your best to work with your team as a whole as we're here to do this together, not solo!    Respect boundaries.
Please respect other players' boundaries if they communicate something or based off their boundaries listings for romance and platonic relationships. You can refer to everyone's boundaries for their characters by checking on the individual character's listing on the masterlist. Please be sure to communicate with your fellow players and be respectful on handling things!    Plotting permissions
Minor / personal character plots are perfectly fine and encouraged! You do not need mods permission to do these things and welcome to do these as you please (so long as it doesn't conflict / derail team focus). If you have an idea that would affect characters group wide or need involvement from NPCs, please contact the mod team so we can help you out! If you wish to kill off your character, please contact the mod team beforehand so we can sort out that plot point and you will become a spectator upon your character's death.    One life.
Each player will only be allowed to play 1 character in the group and will not be allowed to switch out to another one. When applying, please be sure that you are committed to your character as there will be no going back.
